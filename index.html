About

I'm a CS Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech in Blacksburg, Virginia. I am honored to be advised by Dr. Muhammad Ali Gulzar, who has been very supportive of me and my work.

My research covers AI system debugging, AI for code, and program analysis and verification. I want automated tools for code to be provably correct.

Before my Ph.D., I worked at Microsoft, where I focused on improving developer productivity and worked on OneNote development.

I like problems where a compiler, a solver and a model each check the others' work.
