About

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Virginia Tech. My research covers AI system debugging, AI for code, and program analysis and verification. I want automated tools for code to be provably correct.

Before my Ph.D., I worked at Microsoft, where I focused on improving developer productivity and worked on OneNote development.

I like problems where a compiler, a solver and a model each check the others' work.
